- PDF.js (`pdfjs-dist`) — Apache-2.0 — https://github.com/mozilla/pdf.js

The build includes notices generated from the installed dependency packages, plus explicit PDF.js and Readability license files. Review the lockfile and bundled license texts when redistributing this build.
